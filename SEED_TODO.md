### 10. Monetary history, 1971, grade A

**Sentence:** On August 15, 1971, President Nixon suspended the dollar’s convertibility into gold, ending the Bretton Woods gold link.

**Check:** Date and suspension confirmed. FR History: gold window closed evening of 15 August 1971; foreign governments could no longer exchange dollars for gold. - Publisher: FRASER / Papers of Richard M. Nixon

- Quote: `suspend temporarily the convertibility of the dollar into gold`
